# Is Bilt Obsidian worth adding if you already have a rewards card?

Editorial draft. Issuer terms checked November 20, 2026.

For a high-income household, Bilt Obsidian’s $95 annual fee is easiest to justify when grocery or dining spending already clears a housing threshold and earns more than the card you would otherwise use. Start with the dollars you can move each billing cycle, the rewards those purchases already earn, and the Bilt points you would have earned on housing with the $0 Bilt Blue. Household income tells you little about any of those inputs.

## Start with the card you would keep

Obsidian earns 3 points per dollar in your selected category, dining or groceries. The grocery bonus applies to the first $25,000 of qualifying grocery purchases per calendar year. The other category generally earns 1 point per dollar, and qualifying travel earns 2 points per dollar. If you already hold Amex Gold, it earns 4 Membership Rewards points per dollar on qualifying restaurant and U.S. supermarket purchases up to its separate category limits. If you plan to keep Gold, its annual fee and benefits are the same in both scenarios. Compare the purchase rewards you would give up by moving spending to Obsidian.

At 1¢ per Bilt point, Obsidian’s 3x category returns an estimated 3% before its fee. Against an existing 2% card, the extra 1% covers the $95 fee at $9,500 of annual eligible category spending, before any additional housing points or usable credits. Against an existing 4x card with points also worth 1¢, the same switch gives up 1% on those purchases. Housing points or usable credits would need to cover both the lost rewards and the fee.

## What changes for a renter and a homeowner?

These examples use Bilt’s housing-only rewards option. Eligible rent or mortgage payments go through Bilt and draw from a linked bank account. Both Blue and Obsidian can earn a monthly 250-point floor on an eligible Bilt housing payment and reach 0.5 points per housing dollar when qualifying card spending in the billing cycle reaches 25% of the housing payment. Check eligibility and your cycle dates before counting a payment or purchase.

Assume 12 identical billing cycles, 1¢ per Bilt point and per Amex point, qualifying supermarket spend within both caps, and $0 of value for credits, welcome offers and partner promotions. Only the groceries shown move. All other wallet spending and rewards stay the same.

| Household | Practical Blue baseline | What changes with Obsidian | Estimated annual gain after $95 fee |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Renter paying $2,800 per month and spending $900 per month at qualifying supermarkets | Keep groceries on Amex Gold at 4x and earn 250 housing points per month on Blue | Earn 1,400 housing points, up 1,150. Grocery rewards drop by $9 per month, leaving a $2.50 monthly gain before the fee. | −$65 |
| Homeowner paying an eligible $4,000 mortgage and spending $1,100 per month at qualifying supermarkets | Move those groceries from a 2% card to Blue at 1x to earn 2,000 housing points. At this valuation, the added housing points cover the purchase rewards given up versus taking the 250-point floor. | Earn the same 2,000 housing points. Grocery rewards rise by $22 per month versus Blue. | +$169 |

A household with a specific award in mind can use a different point value. Bilt lists World of Hyatt at a 1:1 transfer ratio. Compare the cash rate you would actually pay with the award’s taxes and fees, then divide the savings by the number of points used. At an illustrative 1.5¢ per Bilt point and 1¢ per Amex point, the renter’s Obsidian estimate rises to $166 per year after the fee against the same Blue and Gold setup. Use that higher estimate only if you expect to redeem at that value.

Count credits at what they replace in your budget. For accounts opened before January 1, 2027, Bilt’s terms describe up to $100 in qualifying Bilt Travel hotel credits across two benefit periods. New accounts opened on or after that date receive the $100 Bilt Cash benefit. Earlier accounts generally transition on their first anniversary in 2027, subject to the legacy election. Check your account’s terms before assigning any value to either benefit.
